How LifeCard Works

Consent-driven emergency response connecting three critical parties in seconds.

💳

1. Patient Holds LifeCard

A physical NFC/QR card containing a single secure token. Cardholder maintains real-time consent toggles over what strangers can see.

🆘

2. Helper Scans & Alerts

Any phone camera opens the helper page. Helper can dial 112, call the brother, and share GPS location which alerts the family feed instantly.

🩺

3. Doctor Clinical Bridge

Verified doctors confirm arrival at hospital, receive a 30-minute countdown grant to full history (diabetes, asthma, allergies), and update family.
